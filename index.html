01 · The Problem

Staying hydrated is harder than it should be

  • Heavy to carrySeveral bottles are heavy and take up space.
  • Nowhere to refillWater is hard to find when hiking, playing sports or travelling.
  • Plastic wasteBuying bottled water costs money and creates waste.

Our answer: Fountain Bottle. One bottle that refills itself, so you never have to carry extra water or buy another plastic bottle.

X-ray view · the portal at the base
02 · How It Works

One portal, endless water

The base of the bottle is a portal to a glacier in the Elven Mountains. Water flows in by itself, so the bottle never runs dry, unless the mountain does.

  1. Fill onceFill the bottle the first time to activate the portal.
  2. Turn the ringTwist the ring at the neck to open it. Water rises by itself.
  3. Drink and closeDrink as much as you like, then twist the ring to close.

05 · Customers & Care

Who it's for

Target customers

  • Students
  • Athletes and people who work out
  • Hikers and travellers
  • Busy workers who forget to drink water

Good to know

  • If the mountain runs dry, the bottle pauses until it refills.
  • Water only. No other drinks.
  • Close the ring after every use.
  • Hand wash only. Not dishwasher safe.
